Original wording: dynamic shelves revert to older results after the app was closed for a while** (example: Oprah's newly added picks vanish). Still suspected, not proven: Oprah's source answers from Wikipedia alone when Oprah Daily fails, which can push the newest official picks below older dated ones (order, not loss); localStorage quota errors are swallowed in `cache.ts` (`catch {}`), so a full iPhone storage also reverts silently. Fix applied for (a) and (b) in this branch; ALSO (owner decision 2026-10-28): the storage-full cleanup no longer deletes saved shelf lists (`KEEP_WHEN_STORAGE_FULL` in cache.ts), while "Refresh everything" still clears everything back to initial (not device-tested).
